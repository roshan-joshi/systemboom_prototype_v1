# Celestial reference-led art family — 22 September 2026

Eight original transparent raster masters generated with the built-in OpenAI image generation tool, using the owner-provided `owner-lineup-poster.png` as visual direction. The reference's altered phrases were not adopted. All canonical identifiers, meanings and phrases remain in the unchanged registry and localization catalogs.

Runtime: 64 WebP exports, eight objects × two environment treatments × four optical tiers. These replace the visual-layer lookup only; legacy assets are preserved.

- Signal: 64px export for 16–24px use; Venus/Jupiter/Moon/Mercury use body-focused crops with small entourage removed, plus dedicated sharpening. Sun, meteor paths, comet tail and Saturn's rings keep their full silhouettes.
- Seal: 96px export, full silhouette with tighter framing and mild sharpening.
- Object: 256px export, full entourage with extra breathing room for the field's directional light and contact reflection.
- Event: 512px export, uncropped richer entourage at larger optical occupancy; the existing per-object one-shot motion controls presentation and disposal.
- Solar exports keep saturated colors and dark internal values; Cosmos exports use the same physical identities with a distinct saturation treatment. Runtime light and contact shadows differ by theme.

Build: `node prototype-tests/_build-celestial-family.cjs`.
Source masters, original generation paths, prompts and export parameters: `prototype-evidence/celestial-mastering/art-family/` (local review evidence).

The SVG page environments are original code-authored atmospheric geometry, separate from the raster object artwork. No mascot artwork was changed.
