# Phase 5 candidates — recorded so they are not lost

## "The Field": moments plotted by position in their author's life

Origin: Phase 4.0 Social visual-language exploration (2026-09-10), Direction C.
Rejected as a *feed* by owner decision; recorded here as a candidate for the
Circle of Life (Phase 5) or for a view of one's people across their lifespans.

**The idea.** Stop stacking. Render a set of moments as a chart whose
horizontal axis is *age in life* (0 at the left edge, 105 at the right,
eight faint 15-year columns — the Circle of Life unrolled into the page's
width) and whose vertical axis is clock time. A 22-year-old's moment sits
left; a 61-year-old's sits right; a person's own moments march slowly
rightward as they age. The page itself is the differentiator; nothing on it
needs to shout.

**Why it failed as a social feed.** At 360px the metaphor degrades to
decoration (eight columns of 45px cannot hold text); horizontal scanning
cost is high; it exposes other people's ages spatially and unavoidably
(owner decision Q1 in Phase 4.0: other people show their Circle band only,
never day precision); text-heavy moments read badly in narrow plates.

**Why it may succeed in Phase 5.** The Circle is *already* a life-position
instrument, so the axis is native there; a single person's own life has no
privacy problem; "my people across their lifespans" is a bounded view that
can be opt-in per person and can use bands, not exact positions, for others.
It also gives the Circle a non-pie rendering — the current 3-D pie chart in
the live system is a dashboard widget and should not survive.

**Constraints to carry forward.** Band-precision for others by default;
reduced-motion alternative; light mode must carry the chart by line work and
type; no rainbow band colours — tonal steps of one ink (`--steel` / `--ice`).


## Quiet acknowledgment word for shared health records (banked 2026-09-11, C5.4)

Origin: Phase 4.1 review. HEALTH and PROBLEM moments are records, not stories:
they carry no Respond affordance in Phase 4 and no substitute ships. If a shared
health record ever needs a way for a friend to say "I saw this, I'm here", it
must be a **different word from Respond**, quiet, and without a count on the
reading surface — applause is the wrong response to a blood-pressure reading.
Candidates to evaluate later: "Noted", "With you", a private one-to-one note.
Constraints: Only-me default unchanged; no public tally; privacy carries into the
Circle (patterns are more exposing than single posts). **Not built. Bank only.**


## Same day across your life (banked 2026-09-11, Phase 5 §21 → Phase 5.x)

At DAY resolution a deliberate secondary action could show `10 SEP · across my life`:
every year's 10 September, oldest to newest, using the same Almanac components. The
model already answers it (`visibleMoments` filtered by month and day); the gesture,
its discoverability and its presentation are **not built** so the core drill stays
perfect. Constraints carried forward: owner only (a visitor's Circle has one level);
derived from the viewer-safe set; the same red-budget and language rules; no
"on this day" nostalgia framing — it is a coordinate, not a memory prompt.

## Photo Timeline as a Circle view mode (direction, not built)

The Circle is the primary time scrubber. A photo view can become a content mode at
MONTH / DAY resolution (the Almanac filtered to media, laid out as a grid) instead of
a separate long-term scrubber. One time model.
