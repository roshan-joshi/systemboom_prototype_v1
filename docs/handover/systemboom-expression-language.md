# SYSTEMBOOM — the Expression language (R3 · art direction R3.1 · gravity R3.7 · horizon R3.8 · emotion engine R3.9)

SYSTEMBOOM's own emotional action language: **eighteen** feelings a person can attach to a
Moment, carried by the SYSTEMBOOM mascot. It is not a reaction economy — one viewer holds
at most ONE expression per Moment, nothing is ranked, and no number is ever presented as
status. R3.3 (2026-09-16) added the aggregation layer — the **Boom Lens** and **Human Pulse** (`human-pulse-contract.md`) — so 1 to 10,000 humans summarise without mascot noise, and moved the palette to four colour FAMILIES (warmth · energy · wonder · connection). Interaction accepted 2026-09-15; the art direction was reopened the same day by
R3.1 ("the current expression system looks too dull") and rebuilt as the Quick **Deck** and
the Expression **Library** on the owner's real 3D mascot.

**R3.5 (2026-09-16) — the EMOTION CORE.** The quick six now read without labels: a machined
cutaway on the body reveals a large internal core (heart · sun · laughter-waves + tear ·
starburst · firework · cradled orb), composited onto the owner's untouched render and served
through the existing `RENDERED` slots at every tier (deck, library, all three lens crops).
Selection plays one ≤320ms internal core swell, clipped inside the lens. Evidence:
`prototype-evidence/social-r3-5-emotion-core/01-emotion-core-six.png` (the no-label board).

**Honest status:** the per-expression FACES are still ART ASSET BLOCKED — one pose was
supplied, so all eighteen currently wear the same face. R3.2 then finished the QUICK SIX's
interaction, motion, touch and asset system and tested the art capability by experiment
(there is no 3D renderer and no image-generation model here; Care and Support both need a
closed, non-grinning mouth that 2D cannot synthesise). See **`quick-six-render-briefs.md`**
first, then `expression-render-briefs.md` for the extended twelve, and the judgement board
`prototype-evidence/social-r3-2-signature-expression/01-quick-six-no-label-no-mark.png`.

Companion documents: `expression-asset-contract.md` (assets, poses, motion, the blocked 3D
renders), `moment-conversation-model.md` (Respond · Responses · Reply), `moment-expression-
contract.md` (the R2 data/live contract, still current).

## Why a mascot and not emoji

Unicode emoji are the person's own words and stay exactly that (§60). The mascot language
is the *action* layer: it says how a MOMENT — a person at a point in their life — landed
with you. One tap, one feeling, reversible, attached to the memory rather than to content.

## The eighteen

**Quick six** — one tap from the Moment, instantly understandable, highest art quality:
`Care · Joy · Laugh · Wow · Celebrate · Support`

**The rest** — one affordance away, behind **More**, all eighteen shown in the Library WITH
their names because the names are how the language is learned (R3 §27, §64). They are
ordered by **emotional group**, which is structure and air — never a tab, never a label:

| group | expressions |
|---|---|
| energy | Proud · Speechless |
| warmth | Love · Thanks · Touched · With you |
| thought | Respect · Inspired · Curious · Agree · Thinking · Nostalgia |

R3.1 §14 resolved one duplication: **Surprised** was dropped (it was Wow twice) and
**Nostalgia** took the slot — a life-memory emotion this product needed and had no word for.

Each has exactly one primary human meaning; two expressions may never need their label to
be told apart (§10). The semantic pairs that were deliberately separated:

| pair | difference |
|---|---|
| Care ↔ Touched | Care GIVES warmth (leans in, heart). Touched RECEIVES impact (drawn inward, ripple). |
| Joy ↔ Laugh | Joy is buoyant happiness (lift). Laugh is funny (rocked back 14°, one side-rock). |
| Wow ↔ Inspired | Wow is surprise (recoil, flare). Inspired is aspiration (rises, looks up). |
| Support ↔ With you | Support is steady and protective (planted). With you is empathy and presence (stands beside, calm). |
| Care ↔ Love | Care is warmth offered outward. Love is the stronger, held feeling — distinct in every locale, not one word twice (R3.1 §12). |
| Agree ↔ Respect | Agree is assent to what was said. Respect is recognition of the person. |

**Not in the set, deliberately:** angry, hate, dislike, downvote, mocking, sarcastic (§23) —
a one-tap negative strips human context; a person who disagrees can Respond with words.
**Reserved:** BOOM, once its meaning is proven with people (§24).

## Motion personality

ANTICIPATE → EXPRESS → **MASS IMPULSE** → BOOM PULSE → SETTLE. Shared DNA; since R3.2 the
quick six each carry their own **tempo and gesture** — Care 360ms soft · Joy 300ms light ·
Laugh 400ms playful · Wow 260ms sharp · Celebrate 460ms energetic · Support 380ms grounded —
and their own fuse voice (warm · lift · **wobble** · flare · burst · steady). The rest of the
registry keeps the three energy families (quiet 300 · warm 380 · lively 460ms). Since R3.1
there is also a separate **mass** layer: this
character is a heavy metal bomb, not a rubber emoji. `heavy` barely overshoots and lands hard
(300ms, hard-landing curve); `normal` takes one clean overshoot (360ms); `light` may bound
once (440ms, springier). The Boom Pulse is a single thin **pressure ring** leaving the
character's own edge, mass-scaled (heavy 1.62× · normal 1.85× · light 2.02×) — displaced air,
never a glow and never a Material ripple. Restrained 3D-feeling transforms only — translate,
scale, small rotate; never a spin, never a flip, never arcade motion (§33). The fuse carries
emotional charge rather than an explosion metaphor (§34). Celebrate gets one tiny local
burst, never screen confetti (§15).

**The feed is still.** Nothing animates without interaction — not on scroll, not on
intersection, not when many summaries are on screen (§36, §82, §84, §107). Reduced motion
collapses every keyframe to its final state, and the POSE survives, so the emotion is still
readable with no animation at all (§70).

## Accessibility

Every expression carries a localized semantic name (`expr.*`, all 8 locales), the rail is a
composite radiogroup (arrows move, Enter commits, Escape closes and returns focus), the
selected state reads as an owned seat + Boom notch rather than colour alone (§71, R3.1 §24),
and the commit is announced politely. Animation is decorative and never screen-reader
content (§68). Reduced motion collapses the seat's rise to instant while the state stays
fully legible, and every POSE survives.


## R3.7 — Gravity Expressions (visual / motion language; semantics unchanged)

**Emotion Chamber.** The core lives INSIDE the mascot. At asset level the chamber is a bore
seen obliquely: the machined lip stays, the opening is offset inside it so the shell wall
reads thick on the upper-left and thin on the lower-right, and the core sits deeper, larger,
partly under the wall, in the wall's cast shadow, its light reflecting off the lit far wall.
At component level the chamber is a second layer (`data-sb-core-layer`, the artwork clipped
to the opening) so the core can lag the shell by ~1.5px during a pointer preview — real
depth, never scroll-linked, none under reduced motion. The Boom Lens windows are core-led
(the chamber fills ~40% of the lens; brow, eye edge and teeth still identify SYSTEMBOOM).

**Gravity Dock.** The quick picker is one physical ground, not six tiles: transparent hit
targets (68px desktop / 84px phone), one directional light, contact shadows, a shallow
symmetric arc on desktop (outer characters sit ~10px lower and lean outward), the 3×2 spatial
field on a phone. The chosen character rests inside an orbit ring in its accent, with the Boom
notch. Cosmos is connected through physics — mass, gravity, orbit spacing, inertia, one light
source — never through stars, orbit lines, HUD circles or particles.

**Five states, every expression.**

| state | what happens | time |
|---|---|---|
| REVEAL | the dock opens; the six rise into position, 12ms stagger | ≤180ms |
| PREVIEW | hover / focus / drag: the character rises 4px, the chamber wakes with its own light event (Care fills · Joy radiates · Laugh compresses/releases · Wow contracts→snaps · Celebrate rises · Support closes), the core lags the shell, the label appears | 80–140ms |
| COMMIT | the chamber leaves the character as ONE flight to the Boom Lens while the control plays gesture → core ignition → mass response → Boom Pulse; the dock fades, inert | 250–450ms (tempo band) |
| SETTLE | everything stops; the selected state is static | — |
| RETOUCH | tapping the committed control: a small core + rim response, never the commit again | 160ms |

Reduced motion: no reveal, no parallax, no flight, no fade — final states immediately.

**Expression Field.** More is a field, not a grid: eighteen free-standing characters with
their names beneath, organised in four family bands — warmth · energy · wonder · connection —
separated by air and a hairline, each band's ground faintly warmed by its tone. Never tabs,
never a card around a mascot. The canonical `LIBRARY` order (used by the Spectrum) is unchanged.

**Facial art.** Still ART ASSET BLOCKED (R3.2 §77). The chamber composites are the honest
interim; true per-expression renders replace them file-for-file with no component change.


## R3.8 — Emotion Horizon (the interaction concept)

**One mascot. Six cores.** The picker never shows six copies of the mascot again. Tapping the
Expression control opens a local field growing from the control: ONE vessel mascot (84–110px,
grounded by a contact shadow) with its dormant, empty chamber, and the six emotions as small 3D
CORE OBJECTS on a shallow horizon below it — lit translucent orbs, one directional light, the
emotion's form inside (heart · radiant sun · double wave · faceted blue starburst · contained
ignition · protected orb with embracing arcs). No cards, no buttons, no emoji tray.

**Preview.** Attending a core (hover, focus, or a finger dragging across) lifts it toward the
chamber, wakes the chamber, and puts THAT feeling into the vessel — the vessel renders the
expression (its chamber shows the core) and makes only a small gesture; the label names it.

**Commit (≈300–500ms).** The core accelerates into the chamber (120ms) while the others recede
→ the shell locks it (a short contraction/release) and the vessel performs the expression's own
gesture, fuse and Boom Pulse → the activated chamber collapses into the Boom Lens beside Respond
(200ms) as the field lets go → the lens lands with a thump and one ring. Then nothing moves.
On a phone a tap commits at once; drag-to-preview is optional.

**Core → chamber → lens.** The same object survives every scale: the Boom Lens is now the same
OBLIQUE CHAMBER APERTURE (a rotated ellipse with a metal shell fragment — thick wall upper-left,
thin lower-right), core-led at 22–40px with only a lip, brushed body and lower teeth as the
SYSTEMBOOM cue. Human Pulse, Spectrum and Who Expressed keep their architecture and wear the new
lens. The closed control shows the dormant chamber before a choice and the selected core after —
never a tiny full mascot.

**Emotion Atlas.** "More" keeps the one vessel and shows all eighteen as core objects in four
family bands (warmth · energy · wonder · connection), each named beneath; attending any core
previews it in the same vessel. The extended twelve cores are provisional family orbs carrying
their registry glyph, stated as such.

**Facial art.** Still ART ASSET BLOCKED (R3.2 §77). The cores solve recognition at every scale
without pretending to be faces; true per-expression renders drop into the same vessel slots.


## R3.9 — the Signature Emotion Engine (the emotional event)

Committing an expression is ONE local emotional event belonging to the Moment, never the screen:

CORE AWAKENS → CHARACTER RESPONDS → THE EMOTION ESCAPES INTO LOCAL SPACE → SHELL AND GROUND
RECEIVE THE LIGHT → BOOM IMPULSE → COLLAPSE INTO THE BOOM LENS → COMPLETE STILLNESS.

The escaped emotion is real ENERGY (the chamber's own core drawings rendered free, tight
emission, the key light baked in), staged in true depth — background pieces pass behind the
vessel, foreground pieces travel toward the viewer. Per expression: **Care** (the hero) — one
large dimensional heart toward the viewer with two smaller at other depths; **Joy** — four
controlled rays + warm light travelling the shell; **Laugh** — two rhythmic resonance waves +
one transparent droplet; **Wow** — collapse → one restrained pressure ring + a cool reflection
crossing the metal; **Celebrate** — five embers on believable gravity arcs; **Support** — two
structures closing gently around the orb, energy settling DOWN ("I am here with you"). Light
has a source: the shell receives the emotion's light AT the chamber; the ground a restrained
reflection. The whole event is over inside ~900ms; after settle, ZERO expression animation.

**World Light.** Two light systems. WORLD LIGHT is persistent and subtle (10–14%): Deep Cosmos
casts a cool ambient, Solar Observatory a warm one, on the vessel's rim, contact-shadow tint
and ground pool only — the live seam is the World Wall's atmosphere token (not yet in this
repo; theme material is the shipped truth source). EMOTION LIGHT is short-lived and generated
by the core during interaction. They never mix meanings: a warm World still receives Wow's
blue; no World ever recolours a core.

**Respecting humans.** Scroll is never locked; a second choice snaps the old event clean;
events never stack; reduced motion mounts NO event (tap → the selected chamber is simply
there); retouch stays a 160ms internal response. A future native haptic could map to
anticipation → core lock → Boom impulse (documented seam only — no web-faked haptics, no audio).

**Facial art.** Still ART ASSET BLOCKED — re-verified this round: no image generation exists in
this environment. The event system carries the emotion honestly until the renders land.


## R3.9.1 — the Emotion Signet (the resting lens)

The compact resting state is a SIGNET, not a picture of the chamber: the exact core object the
person touched, seated in the oblique aperture (the chamber's own −12° ellipse) on a dark seat
well, ringed by machined theme metal — dark steel in Deep Cosmos, warm brass in Solar
Observatory — with the emotion's light on the top arc of the rim and the scarce Boom-red
ownership segment machined into the lower rim. Object permanence is complete: the orb chosen on
the horizon is the orb locked in the chamber is the orb that returns beside Respond. The dormant
control is the same ring around the empty aperture. Focus is one circular accent ring — the
rectangular browser outline is gone. The optical lens crops (`{id}-lens-*.webp`) remain built
and exported as the slot for the true facial renders.
