## Notes on how these arose (not a finding, a cause)

Two structural causes explain almost every row above, and the Bible should inherit both:

1. **AGENTS.md's supersession discipline is real but one-directional.** Every supersession this
   audit checked is recorded in AGENTS.md — dated, with the invariant restated and the affected
   test named. What AGENTS.md does *not* do is propagate the change into `docs/`. The only pass
   that explicitly logged a **documentation** supersession is S5/S6 (composer body order) — and
   that one was actioned in exactly **one of five** affected files.

2. **The Phase-4 freeze froze the documents alongside the code.** AGENTS.md names seven handover
   docs plus three design docs as part of the *frozen accepted Social reference*. Freezing them
   made them hard to correct when later owner-directed rounds (R2, R3, R3.1, R3.3, R3.7, R3.8,
   R3.9, S5/S6) changed what they describe. **C-1, C-2, C-3, C-6 and C-7 all sit inside that
   frozen set.**

The corpus also contains its own governing rule and does not follow it —
`systemboom-navigation-final.md:5–6`: *"Where an older document disagrees, this one and the
accepted, tested behaviour win — **and the older document is corrected**."*
Restated for i18n at `docs/i18n/architecture.md:5–7` and for devices at
`s7-device-responsive-contracts.md:4–6`. In practice the older document was left uncorrected in
every case above except C-1's single file.
